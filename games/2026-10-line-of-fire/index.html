Line of Fire

Hand injuries are rising across our industry. Most of them happen on jobs people have done hundreds of times, and plenty happen before the main task starts or after it has finished.

This game is about one question. Ask it before every task.

  1. Eight moments from an ordinary working day, from hitching up to packing away.
  2. Mark the hands. Each picture shows several places a hand could be. Mark every one that is in the line of fire.
  3. Make the call. Choose what you would do.
  4. See what happens when something moves.

This one is not a competition. There is no prize, no Top 10 and no clock, so take your time. You get a score out of 800 and feedback on every answer.

A simplified game. It is not a substitute for your training, the risk assessment or company procedures.

Demo for testing: nothing is recorded, and your answers stay on this device.
