Fungi Detective

Autumn is when the fungi show themselves. You've ten trees to check before the winter storms, beside 11kV and 33kV pole lines and a 132kV tower line.

  1. Find it. Something is growing on each tree. Tap it on the picture to take a closer look.
  2. Name it. Pick the fungus from four choices.
  3. Categorise it. Step back and give the tree its utility category, A to D. Go by the nearest part of the canopy, not the stem, and use the scale bar. The vicinity zone depends on the voltage.
  4. Decide. Leave it, monitor it, reduce it or fell it. Think about what the fungus does to the tree, and whether the tree can reach the line.

Each tree is worth 100 points: 40 for naming the fungus, 20 for the category and 40 for the right action, with half marks for a reasonable second choice. The best possible score is 1000.

One attempt each. Equal scores are split by the faster time.
